CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Clear, transparent rules regarding subscription cancellations, digital course access, and refunds.

1. Digital Products & Subscriptions

Traders Hub offers immediate access to proprietary digital intellectual property, recorded lecture libraries, live analysis desks, and community discussions. Because access is granted immediately upon transaction completion, monthly desk subscriptions and recorded courses are generally non-refundable once content has been accessed.

2. Duplicate or Erroneous Payments

In the event that you were charged twice for the same transaction due to a network glitch or payment gateway timeout, the duplicate charge will be refunded 100% in full. Please report duplicate transactions to our support team with your payment transaction ID within 48 hours.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed via our payment gateway (PayPal) and credited back directly to the original source payment method (Bank Account, Card, or PayPal Account) within 5 to 7 working days as per standard banking procedures.
