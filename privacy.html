PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026. How we collect, safeguard, and process your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, subscribe to our trading desks, or enroll in courses on Traders Hub, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Authentication data: Google OAuth identifier (sub claim) and verified profile details.
  • Payment records: Transaction ID, order ID, amount, and timestamp. (We never store your raw credit card numbers or security credentials; all online transactions are processed via secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways such as PayPal).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide access to your member dashboard, VIP rooms, and community desks.
  • Process course enrollments and subscription renewals.
  • Send transaction receipts and critical security alerts.
  • Provide customer support via Telegram, WhatsApp, and email.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption, cryptographically signed HTTP cookies, and secure session management. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third-party advertisers.
